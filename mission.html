Our Mission

Reason 1

We want people to leave this site feeling like sustainability is a possibility for them. A lot of the content we came across while doing research made us feel overwhelmed or like we weren’t doing enough. To combat that feeling we made it our goal to leave users encouraged and with real recommendations that are student-wallet friendly

Reason 2

We also want to use this site to highlight small local businesses that dont get the recognition the deserve. A lot of them are owned by people of color and are genuinely making a positive impact in their communities. We hope that by putting them on here more people can find them and choose to support them over bigger chains that don’t give back in the same way.

Reason 3

We hope RootedLocal helps people see that sustainability and community are connected. Taking care of the environment and investing in the people around you aren’t two completely separate things. When you shop local, reduce waste, or make more intentional choices about what you buy, you’re doing both at the same time and its something worth advocating for.

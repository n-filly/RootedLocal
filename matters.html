Why it Matters

Reason 1

We made RootedLocal because we kept seeing the same problem, people our age want to do something about the environment but struggle with high costs or not enough time. We also noticed that a lot of small businesses in our area, especially ones owned by POC don’t get the support they deserve. So we decided to build something that tries to fix both of those things. The idea is to make sustainable living feel less like a chore and allowing you to feel more connected to where you actually live without needing to break the bank.

Reason 2

We’re not gonna pretend we had it all figured out either. A lot of us didn’t really think about sustainability that much until we started to look into it. Once we did I was surprised by how many small businesses in our own neighborhood are already doing the work, and how easy it is to support them without spending more money. Low income communuities, and communities of color face more pollution, less access to healthy foods, and even less support.

Reason 3

The part that made us feel better about all of this is that the most effective changes are also the cheapest ones. By buying secondhand, supporting local businesses, and cutting down on single use items you’ve already began to make a lasting impact. None of it requires spending more money than you already do. It mostly just takes knowing where to look, which is exactly what we were trying to figure out when we created RootedLocal.
